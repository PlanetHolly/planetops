Your $20 garment fear — answered

Your instinct to stop me was right. The answer is that 25% is safe.

Iron & Velvet is a real VIP contract account. Here is what you already charge them, versus what the proposal would charge:

At 25%, you land at or below a VIP contract price at every tier. And read the third column: at today's 10% you are charging walk-up customers LESS than your contract account. That is the actual mistake — not the 25%.

But there is a structural lesson in I&V that changes HOW we should do this. Its builder has an explicit column, Blank Markup % = 0.10. I&V marks the garment up only 10% — and puts the margin in a large decoration Sales-Profit adder instead ($16.40/unit at 12pc).

So there are two ways to reach the same total price: mark up the garment, or mark up the decoration. Your own VIP model does the latter. This is a real architectural choice and it deserves a decision, not a default. It matters competitively: customers know what blanks cost, so where you put the margin changes what you look expensive at.

My recommendation: do not flip the 25% yet. The number is safe. The architecture is an open question, and it is worth one more day to get right.
