What changed today, and what did NOT.

CHANGED: the garment markup, 10% → 25%. In Printavo this is one column: Product Markup % = 110.0 → 125.0.
NOT CHANGED: every decoration price cell is byte-identical to the set built and verified on 2026-07-10. I did not touch your prices.
Why the markup, and why 25%. The blank is 52% of an apparel invoice and was marked up only 10% — nine cents on the dollar. That is why apparel earns a 37.5% Sales-Profit rate against the 45% the business needs, and why revenue grew 24% while Sales Profit fell 7%.

A bonus nobody expected: at 10% the $0.60 floor bound on cheap blanks, so the rule max(10% × blank, $0.60) could not be expressed as a single Printavo column — your own audit called that a Tier-1 blocker. At 25% the floor never binds (the cheapest real blank is $3.41). A flat 125.0 is exactly the rule. The blocker disappears.
