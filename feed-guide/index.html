Planet Apparel

Feeding the system

Drop a document. It reaches whatever needs to know about it. This page is where to put things, and how to be sure they landed.

Where things go

Everything lives under Dropbox/PlanetApparel/PlanetIQ/Feed/. Pick by what the thing is:

Payroll/
Payroll summaries, OT screenshots, outside-vendor payments. Kelly, each pay period.
Monthly_Financials/
P&L, KPI tracker, invoice tracker. Kelly, month end. Always pull accrual, never cash.
Production_Times/
Photos of job time sheets, the month-end print times report. Jean's team.
General/
The catch-all. Repairs, equipment, one-off costs, anything notable.
Intake/  — the new one
Anything at all, plus a note saying what it is. Vendor orders, POs, bills, reports. Sorted by intent, not type.

The one that bites

Do not drop in Dropbox/Apps/PlanetIQ-Feed/

That folder belongs to the robot. It looks almost identical and it is not where people put things. On 15 July two payroll files were hand-saved there and both landed at 0 bytes. The files existed, so nothing alarmed, and that pay period was simply gone until someone went looking.

Never download straight into Dropbox

Dropbox can sync the empty placeholder before the download finishes, and you are left with a file that looks real in the folder and holds nothing. Save to your Desktop first, then drag it in. That is what produced the two empty payroll files.

Using Intake

Intake is the interesting one. You say what the thing is, and it goes wherever that belongs. Two files:

  • the document, e.g. sns_invoice_1.pdf
  • a note beside it, same name, ending .note.txt, e.g. sns_invoice_1.note.txt

One line in the note is plenty. Say what it is and which job, if there is one:

  • SNS Activewear order for Blink, job #1234, 3 invoices
  • PO for job #5678, from Bear Designz
  • shipping invoice $579.46, UPS weekly bill
  • June Google Analytics spend

Several files that are really one thing, like a three-invoice order, go in together with a single note. They are read as one.

No job number is fine

A UPS weekly bill covers many jobs and has no single one. That is correct, not a gap. Just say what it is.

Then say /feed

Nothing moves on its own. In Claude, say /feed (or just mention you dropped files in PlanetIQ). It reads each new file, works out what it says, and routes it. Anything it is unsure about it holds and asks about rather than guessing.

Making sure it actually arrived

A file existing is not proof the data arrived. To check the recurring feeds are genuinely current:

python3 ~/Dropbox/PlanetApparel/_Skills/feed/scripts/check_expectations.py

It reports PASS or FAIL per feed, and it treats an empty file as FAIL, not as arrived. That is the whole point: it tells you the difference between "nobody sent it" and "something arrived and it was hollow".

Nothing you drop is moved or deleted. Files stay where you put them.
