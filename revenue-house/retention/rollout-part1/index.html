The Contract

THE CALLS ARE
THE JOB

The emails are automatic. The calls are mandatory — they're the personal layer that makes us us.

After EVERY call: mark the Streak task DONE, and comment on the box: CONNECTED / LEFT VOICEMAIL / WRONG NUMBER + one line.

The system reads emails automatically. It is blind to phones. Your log is its eyes — no log, and the machine thinks nothing happened.
