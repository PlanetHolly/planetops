5 · Two-stage combos & notifications confirmed by Jean

The real two-stage jobs (4)

  • 1. Screen-print → in-house post-production
  • 2. Screen-print → woven labels (outsourced) → in-house finishing (common #inmix)
  • 3. Embroidery (outsourced) → in-house post-production
  • 4. Screen-print → embroidery (outsourced) (rare)

NOT two-stage: DTF. Stage change = the Printavo status change itself.

Finishing order: hang-tag → fold → bag → barcode → package (outsourced services return first).

Emails to cut / keep
