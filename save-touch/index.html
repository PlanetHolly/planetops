The Save Touch

The system watches every repeat customer's ordering rhythm. When a good customer quietly slips past their usual reorder timing, it puts ONE task on their retention box — for you. You do the human part. It does the watching, the timing, and the math. A machine never emails a customer; every touch goes out by your hand, from your inbox.

🛑 The pause signal It's peak season. If anything feels off — wrong customer, weird timing, too many tasks — say "PAUSE THE SAVE TOUCHES" in the team chat. One click freezes the whole system. We'd rather pause ten times than annoy one good customer.

How the machine thinks

So you know why a task appears — and why one doesn't:

🟢 Active→ 🟡 At-Risk→ Save touch (you)→ New Sale box→ Paid→ 🟢 Active again

…or, if the save fails after both touches: 🔴 Inactive (human call only)

  • Every night the system recomputes each repeat customer's real ordering rhythm from Printavo delivery dates (not invoice dates — a batch order delivering all summer reads as active, correctly).
  • Slip past ~1.25× your usual cadence → the box moves to 🟡 AT-RISK.
  • Hourly, business hours only, the system queues at most 3 new tasks total — a trickle, never a flood.
  • Anyone with an open deal (live quote or order in progress) is skipped automatically. Event customers are skipped. Already-touched customers are skipped.
  • The moment a save turns into a deal and gets paid, the customer graduates back to ACTIVE automatically. The loop closes without anyone dragging boxes.
