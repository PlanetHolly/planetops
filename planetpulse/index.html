PlanetPulse

The lifeline of every project — how it flowed through our systems, and what we actually made. One place. One truth.

The short version:  For years we shipped great work without ever seeing the real result. PlanetPulse fixes that. It quietly pulls together Printavo, our CRM, production time, payments, and shipping, then tells us the true profit on every project — automatically. This page shows how it works, and the few things we each do to keep it honest.
1

What it is

PlanetPulse is the source of truth for every converted project — every quote that became a real order. The moment money, materials, and time move, it records them against that job and builds the complete financial picture: revenue, real revenue, costs, margin, impressions, and how it all rolled up for the month.

It's also the pulse of the month as a whole — in-house vs. outsourced mix, collected vs. outstanding, margin health, specialty-ink load, and revenue by customer, method, and salesperson. These are the numbers that feed every decision we make.

2

Under the hood

It looks like a simple summary email. Underneath, it's a genuinely sophisticated machine — several systems talking to each other on their own, every hour:

Printavo API

Reads every invoice, payment, expense, fee, and status in real time.

Google Sheets

The living tracker — one tab per month, formulas doing the math.

Streak CRM

Joins each job to the customer's retention status and tier.

Dropbox

Feeds production-time captures from the floor into the cost engine.

Automation (n8n)

The conductor — routes, matches, calculates, and emails, hands-free.

Profit engine

Turns actual press time into real, per-job production cost.

How in-house production cost is calculated

This is the number we never had before. For in-house jobs, PlanetPulse tracks the actual time each step took — screen print plus post-production, or embroidery then fold-and-bag — and applies our rate to that time.

Faster than expected → lower production cost → better margin. Slower → higher cost. The margin in your summary reflects what really happened on the floor, not a guess. As more time is captured, the picture only gets sharper.

4

What we each do to keep it honest

It's light — most of it you already do. But every number above is only as good as what goes in.

1 · Expenses — within 24 hours

Everyone
When you order blanks, materials, or send a PO, enter the expense in Printavo within 24 hours. PlanetPulse records it toward that project's real cost. Late expenses mean wrong margins.

2 · Shipping — before the order's final status

Rosa / Jean
An automation captures shipping as an expense once an order hits its final (shipped / delivered) status — so the cost must be in before then. In the Text Blaze description you only need [SHIPPING] — no need to type FedEx, UPS, etc.

3 · Payments — use the new /pay Text Blaze

Everyone
For any additional payment beyond the normal project prepayment (say a customer adds 10 more bandanas), edit the payment and use /pay so it's added to the project. For Terms and Partial payments (first or final), put the /pay tag in the payment description.

4 · Hashtags — only three things

Sales
Tag only what the system genuinely can't see:
#sh#cc your initials  +  #in#out#inmix#outmix the revenue bucket  +  #rush only if there are rush fees

You do not tag method, ink, new vs. return, VIP, sublimation, DTF, promo, apparel, or bandana — PlanetPulse reads all of it on its own. (Heat Applied Products + #in already means in-house DTF; water-based ink is read straight from the fee.)

5 · Reprints

Everyone
Keep logging reprints under "Reprint for Planet Apparel." Those are handled correctly.

See something wrong?

Chat Holly directly and it gets fixed. Catching an error is exactly how we keep PlanetPulse trustworthy.
