Why are the near days shaded? They sit inside the minimum lead (15 business days). Before the lead existed, the next open day was simply the first green day, and a green day next week would get promised and then fill past cap with work already in motion. Shaded days are not quotable to a client, whatever their color. They can still be printed on. Hiding the shading changes only the look; the next open day is computed the same way.
Wiggle room = minutes on jobs whose production due date is later than the print day (by at least 1 business day). They can move later without touching the client. · Expedite to move = production due is day-of, but the client due date leaves at least 3 business days, so the job can move if we pay for expedited shipping. · Everything else on the day is locked. Tap a day to see the jobs. Slack is about dates only: blanks, screens and the changeover cap still have to be checked.
