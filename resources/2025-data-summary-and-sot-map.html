B. Production Cost → the Master Curve

  • Rate: $140 / productive press hour (winter-idle-adjusted; supersedes old $225). = $284K production pool ÷ ~2,050 winter-adjusted annual press-busy hrs.
  • $284K production pool (2025): labor $179K + consumables $42K + energy $15K + equipment $2K + production rent share $45K.
  • Master Curve formula: JobTime(min) = (8 + 12×colors) + print_rate[colors]×qty → cost/unit = (JobTime ÷ 60) × $140 ÷ qty.
  • Print rates (min/unit): apparel color-dependent (1c .34 · 2c .51 · 3c .58 · 4c .76 · 5c 1.24 — driven by press passes); bandana flat single-pass (1c .33 · 2c .35 · 3c .40 · 4c .42).
  • ⏳ Pending: fold the 2026 +5% crew raise (+$9,334/yr) and the off-books Venmo production support into the rate numerator.

C. Sales Profit Layer (Overhead + Profit)

  • Overhead anchor: ~$69.2K/mo (~$830K/yr) — production-stripped; includes fair owner pay ($112K, detail in private vault) + the 2026 +5% leadership/sales raises.
  • Target profit: $13K/mo ($156K/yr) — Level 2 "Build" (clears ~$300K debt in ~3 yrs + reserve).
  • Total SP pool: ~$986K/yr.
  • Option C allocation (LOCKED 2026-05-25):
    • Outsourced lines carry ~$257K overhead via markup; in-house carries $573K overhead + $156K profit = $729K.
    • Levers: in/out overhead 69/31 · in-house overhead 78/22 bandana/apparel · in-house profit 75/25 bandana/apparel.
    • Per-unit SP (2025 volume, AVERAGES): Bandana $3.17/u ($564K) · Apparel $6.49/u ($165K).
    • Minimum order 48u (12/24 VIP-gated). Buffer ~5% (noise-only).
    • ⏳ Pending: shape the per-unit SP to decline with quantity (average holds).

Part 3 — Gaps & Strategy (Eliminate Human Error)

Principle: every metric has ONE source of truth and ONE owner; automate the pull where possible; the yearly refresh (top of this doc) is a checklist, not a from-scratch rebuild.

  1. 1. QB chart-of-accounts mis-mapping (Kelly). The "Promo" service item (and ~$170K of other items) posts to a generic "Sales" account, so ~21% of revenue is un-departmentized and the P&L department split reads wrong (Merchandise shows ~$0 when it was really ~$202K). Fix: remap "Promo" + the stray items to their proper department income accounts → the P&L self-classifies going forward.
  2. 2. Printavo tag hygiene (Jean / PMs) — Exhibit A for the 2026-05-27 data meeting. Product + production-type tags are unreliable: 64% of bandana orders have NO production-type tag, #bandana is over-applied (attached to ~$2.5M of order value vs $928K real bandana revenue), typos (#badana / #outsoursed), the combined #a&m. This is why we now use QuickBooks for all product totals and treat Printavo as order-shape-only. Fix: enforce the standard tags at quote creation; the SKU_Dictionary.md is the standard. (Won't change that QB stays the SoT for totals — but clean tags make Printavo trustworthy for the order-level slices QB can't give.)
  3. 3. Blank-COG single source. Blank costs are scattered across the Printavo catalog + vendor invoices with no clean home. Fix: establish one maintained blank-cost table.
  4. 4. Press-time capture. Currently manual (screenshots of Printavo schedule). Fix: PlanetOps Writeback becomes the single source for setup/print times once live.
  5. 5. This document. Refreshed yearly = the antidote to "starting from scratch." It's the index; the detail lives in the cited source files.
