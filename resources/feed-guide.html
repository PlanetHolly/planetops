PLANETIQ — FEED GUIDE
How we keep PlanetIQ current — what to drop, where, and when.

PlanetIQ is only as accurate as the data behind it. Keeping it current is simple — four folders, one rule.

The one rule

Drop the file in the right folder. Don't rename it. Don't sort it. Then tell Terminal it's there. That is the entire job — PlanetIQ handles the rest.

The folders

Payroll

PlanetApparel / PlanetIQ / Feed / Payroll
Owner: Kelly  ·  Every pay period
  • The payroll summary report
  • A screenshot of any OT paid outside the normal run
  • A screenshot of any outside payment for someone's time — for example, production-support payments sent through Rosa

This folder holds the full labor picture for the period. If it paid for someone's time, it belongs here.

Monthly Financials

PlanetApparel / PlanetIQ / Feed / Monthly_Financials
Owner: Kelly  ·  Every month-end close
  • Profit & Loss by Month
  • The KPI Tracker
  • The Invoice Tracker

Production Times

PlanetApparel / PlanetIQ / Feed / Production_Times
Owner: Jean's team  ·  As jobs run + month-end
  • As each job finishes — a photo of the time sheet (the setup + print times the team already writes by hand)
  • At month-end — the "Month-End Actual Print Times" report: the month's logged actual times, pulled into one file

Logging jobs as they run keeps PlanetIQ live; the month-end report is the clean record that closes the month.

General — the catch-all

PlanetApparel / PlanetIQ / Feed / General
Owner: Anyone  ·  As needed — no schedule
  • Anything PlanetIQ should know that doesn't fit the other three — a major shop repair, an equipment change, a one-off cost, a process change, anything notable.

Drop a note, a photo, or a document — or just tell Terminal what happened. When in doubt, it goes here.

Two examples

Kelly Month-end close — May

  1. Export from QuickBooks: the May Profit & Loss by Month, the updated KPI Tracker, and the Invoice Tracker.
  2. Drop all three into Feed / Monthly_Financials. No renaming.
  3. Send a quick message: "May financials are in PlanetIQ."

Done — that's the whole task.

Jean Production times — every job + month-end

  1. A job runs. The press operator writes setup + print time on the job sheet — exactly as today.
  2. As sheets are completed, photograph them and drop the photos into Feed / Production_Times.
  3. At month-end, run the "Month-End Actual Print Times" report and drop it in the same folder.
  4. Send a quick message: "May production times are in PlanetIQ."

Done — the paper never leaves the floor, and PlanetIQ still gets the numbers.
