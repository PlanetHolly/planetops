Bandana Blanks — Ordering Workflow

For Malia · the Bandana Order Queue lives in the bandana inventory sheet (tab: Order Queue).

When a bandana order is paid, Printavo moves it to PAID / TERMS – BLANKS TO ORDER and it appears at the top of the Order Queue. Your job: check stock, pull or order the blanks, and move the Printavo status so Jean can see it's handled.

The steps

  1. Read the order. Check the Production Due date — a tight date means order now; a far-out date means you have time to wait. Note the Qty Needed.
  2. Pick the Color (yellow dropdown). The sheet instantly fills On Hand (live), Enough? (✓ Have enough / ⚠ ORDER), and the Vendor. The glance panel at the top also shows on-hand by color — red means low.
  3. Decide — pull or order:
    BLANKS TO PULL FROM INVENTORY — we have enough. Set this status in Printavo. In Blanks Notes write: Goods being pulled from inventory.
    AWAITING BLANKS — you're ordering from the vendor (Tans Club = Import, Carolina Creative = USA-Made, Maiwa = Organic). Set this status in Printavo.
  4. Fill the yellow cells when ordering: Order (doz) — pieces auto-calculate — Cost / Unit, PO #, Blanks Arrive (date all blanks land), and the Blanks Notes (format below).
  5. Log the blanks expense in Printavo within 24 hours, then tick Expense Logged ✓. This feeds our profit numbers — never skip it.
  6. As blanks come in, move the Printavo status along: PARTIALLY / RECEIVED → BLANKS COUNTED IN – READY FOR PRODUCTION. When it's counted in, the queue row greys out — done, but still visible.

Blanks Notes — match Jean's format

Blanks Notes (if ordered) 1) Date ALL blanks will arrive by: 6/22/26 2) Any blanks to pull from in-house?: none 3) Ordered from vendor(s): 50 doz / 600 units 4) Tracking (if applicable): n/a

Pulling from stock instead? Just: Goods being pulled from inventory.
