Part 2 — Repeat Customers (Retention)

A customer who has placed two or more paid orders. Goal: watch their buying rhythm and protect it. Reach out before they slip, save them when they do.

The system monitors cadence automatically once the band-updater is live. Until then, the PM tracks manually using the customer's last order date.

After Every Completed Order — Quality Check

TaskTimingHow
Quality CheckWeek 1 after deliveryT1: personal call. T2: email. T3: skip. Script: ^qc_repeat. Check that the last review request was not sent in the last couple of days before sending.

Active — On Rhythm

The customer is ordering within their normal buying pattern. No proactive outreach needed beyond the post-order QC.

Event customers: fire a proactive pre-season reach-out 2–3 months before their known event window opens. Do not wait for them to reach out.

At Risk — Past Their Normal Rhythm

The customer has gone quiet longer than usual. Two touches, then move on.

TouchT1T2T3
Touch 1 (at entry into At Risk)Personal call within one week. Script: ^atrisk1 talking points.Personal email within two weeks. Script: ^atrisk1.Skip.
Touch 2 (midpoint of At Risk window)A different shape — follow-up call or value-adding email. Not a repeat of Touch 1.Templated follow-up. Script: ^atrisk2.Skip.
No reply after Touch 2Move to Inactive.
If the customer tells you something in conversation — a new date, a change in plans — update the Next Projected Order Date on their Streak box and move them back to Active manually. A real conversation always overrides the system's math.

Inactive — Past the Save Window

Touch 2 did not recover them. One heavy touch, then deprioritize.
