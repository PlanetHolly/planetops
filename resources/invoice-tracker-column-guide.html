Invoice Tracker — Column Guide

What every column means, where its data comes from, and how the tracker fills itself. Built for month-end close so per-job profit, in-house %, and collections are answered in one place — without manual entry.

Trigger 1 · Status

When an invoice hits "Paid / Terms"

The workflow pulls that invoice from Printavo and writes (or refreshes) its row — customer, totals, payments, product, SKU, color count, impressions, quantity. Partial payments update the same row; no more "-A / -B" split lines.

Trigger 2 · Expense

When an expense is entered in Printavo

Blanks, materials, outsourced PO cost, and shipping flow into the COGS columns automatically. Total COGS, Real Revenue, and margin recalculate themselves the moment a cost lands.
