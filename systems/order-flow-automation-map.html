🛣️ Two ways to run a quote — pick by tier

A quote can be worked two ways, chosen by the Streak stage. The automated path is hands-off but fragments; the manual path is personal but needs a human.

 Automated pathManual path
Streak stage"Automations in Printavo""Quote Sent (manual)" → 1st–4th Check-In Sent
Who sendsPrintavo chase, hands-off (from messages.printavo.com)PM personally, in-thread (TextBlaze from their inbox)
Best forT3 / low-valueT1 / T2 / big brands
Next PM actionnone until the Archived email (= didn't proceed)PM works it actively

Why the automated path is T3-only: every status change sends a brand-new email with a new subject line — so a big brand gets 5–10 disconnected emails they can't keep in one thread (reads as spammy). Combined with the chase-firing-during-a-reply flaw, the automated chase is only safe for low-value quotes.

🎯 Target state: once every rep's email is connected, Streak-triggered emails that send from the rep's own inbox (one thread, personal) replace the Printavo chase for everyone — select a Streak status → the customer gets a personal, threaded email automatically. (Build candidate: Streak automations or n8n→Gmail send-as. Owner: Chandler/Systems.)

🔀 In-house vs Outsourced — the production fork (tagged in Printavo)

Every paid job routes by its Printavo tag — #in (decorated in-house) or #out (sent to a vendor), plus mixes (#inmix / #outmix, where it's printed here then sent out — or vice-versa — for a second operation like woven labels or embroidery). ⚠️ Data gap: Printavo knows the route (the #in/#out tag + the imprint reads "In-House Production"), but the Streak "In-house / Outsourced" field is not being populated on the box. Fix: have Pass 2a copy the tag into the field — it already pulls the order, so it's a small add.

🎯 What jumps out for the redesign

  1. The "Paid / Blanks To Order" status fires 5 automations at once — Front-of-House check, Jean, Luis, Daily Sales Tracking, Power Scheduler. The v3 plan splits these (QC email → Blanks Ordered; scheduler/film → QC Passed).
  2. Two review triggers — one on Order Shipped (OFF), one on Delivered/Picked Up (ON). Keep one anchor, retire the other.
  3. 4 Automations.io bridges, not 3 — Chase Sequence, Daily Sales Tracking, Review Request, and the art-approval email BCC.
  4. Two payment emails on paid-in-full say different next steps ("begin art" vs "now in production") — sequencing to reconcile.
  5. Voice: customer copy is friendly but inconsistent (emoji-heavy, varied sign-offs) — a single voice pass would tighten it.
  6. ON/OFF confirmed only for the list-view set; grab the list page to verify the rest. Known OFF: In-Production customer notice, Order-Shipped review relay.

👀 Seen in the wild — live examples (6/29)

  1. The chase fires during an active conversation. On Quote #27332 the customer replied asking for a 2-color comparison, Shara answered him personally — and 3 days later the "Checking In! — awaiting your approval" follow-up still auto-fired. The status-based chase can't see an email reply. Top fix.
  2. Customer emails send under the PM's name (e.g. "Shara Hildebrand"), not a generic address — so they read as personal. Keep that in the voice pass.
  3. A no-discount conflict is baked into a template. The internal "Archive Quote" email tells the PM to "email a discount code for new quote" on the annual check-in — contradicts the standing no-discount rule. Decide + edit the copy.
  4. The full chase→archive funnel works as designed (Quote #27197 ran the complete sequence to a clean archive) — the issue is only the missing reply-detection above.
