Bandana Quote Bot · System Map

What the Bandana
Quote Bot Does

Intended behavior · current build · 2026-07-07
A customer fills out the Get a Quote form. Five minutes later, one of three things happens: the bot emails them a price, drafts a quote for Shara to review, or asks one quick question — then quietly sets up the Streak box and the Printavo quote behind the scenes. It never sends anything before the 5-minute wait, and it never auto-sends anything outside a tight, safe lane.

The Gate
3 outcomes

① Send

Clean & in-scope: Import/Classic blank · qty 48–999 · 1–6 colors · zip present
  • Emails the customer the all-inclusive price ladder
  • Archives the form + labels it Auto-Quoted
  • Builds the Streak box → stage Quote Sent (Auto)
  • Fills the Printavo quote (plastisol 1–3c today)
Fully automatic

② Ask

One thing missing that only the customer can answer: colors, quantity, or zip
  • Sends one short question
  • Re-runs automatically on their reply
  • Nothing quoted until it's answered
Fast-follow — not built yet

③ Hold

Needs a human: USA/Organic blank · qty ≥ 1,000 (T1) · under 48 · art looks like more colors than stated · rush + high value · anything unclear
  • No auto-send
  • Drafts a quote in Shara's mailbox
  • Creates a task so Shara finishes + sends it
Draft only

What the Email Says

Send path — the customer-facing quote
Your {Company} bandana pricing — Planet Apparel
Hi {First}, thanks for reaching out to Planet Apparel. We'd love to make your {color} bandanas with a {N}-color print.
Here's your preliminary pricing with everything included — no separate setup, screen, or shipping fees:
500 bandanas: $4.99 each (your quantity)
600 bandanas: $4.85 each
750 bandanas: $4.54 each
Most customers bump up once they see the per-piece price drop.
(If no artwork was attached:) Don't have artwork yet? Browse our 21 ready-to-print templates…
If this looks good, just reply and we'll put together your formal quote and review your artwork.

Hold path uses a request subject and a short "we're putting your pricing together" note — no numbers, since a human will finish it.
