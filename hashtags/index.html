Planet Apparel · Team Standard

Printavo Hashtag Standard

Just two or three tags per job — that's it. PlanetPulse pulls or figures out everything else on its own, so you only tag the few things it genuinely can't see.

Why so few:  Every extra tag is one more place to mistype or forget. So we only tag what the system can't recover from the invoice tracker, Printavo, or Streak. Apply these at quote creation, exactly as shown, all lowercase.

1 · Production Mode

Required · pick one

You're choosing which revenue bucket the job counts toward.

#inMade entirely in-house
#outFully outsourced to a vendor
#inmixCombination — majority in-house
#outmixCombination — majority outsourced

2 · Your Initials

Required · pick yours
#shShara
#ccCortney

3 · Rush

Only when a rush FEE was charged
#rushOnly when an actual rush / expediting FEE is on the invoice — not just because the customer wanted it fast, and not shipping-expedite fees. This way a real rush never slips by unflagged.

You no longer tag these

PlanetPulse already reads them, so please stop: decoration method, water-based / specialty ink (read from the ink fee, and auto-applied to organic), DTF / Heat Applied (Heat Applied + #in = in-house DTF), product type (bandana/apparel/promo), new vs. repeat customer, USA/organic/import, and VIP. All pulled from the invoice, Printavo category, SKU, fees, or Streak automatically.

The rules

  • Two tags on every job — one production mode + your initials. Add #rush only when a rush fee is charged.
  • Type them exactly, all lowercase. A typo = a job that won't classify.
  • Tag at quote creation, not later — PlanetPulse reads them the moment the job hits Paid / Terms.

Examples

In-house bandanas, Shara's job → #in  #sh
Outsourced promo, Cortney's job → #out  #cc
Majority-in-house apparel with a rush fee, Shara → #inmix  #sh  #rush
